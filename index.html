[ p ] press[ p ] holdturn / push [ v ]2× = double-click› then[ s ] [ z ] = a dual half’s mk4 knobs
jacks: in / out numbers are single mode · default = what the module maps when you choose it02 / 02 · the atlasorange = hold
01matrix mixercv · mixer
a 6-in, 4-out mixer built for cv (audio is fine too). static mixes, cv-mapped moving mixes, or cv generation: automate the input and output offsets and it becomes four macro cvs for the rest of the patch. outputs can be quantized.
jacksin 1–6 mix inputs · out 1–4 mix outputs
defaultno default mappings
02augustus loopaudio · delay
expert sleepers’ tape delay plug-in, stereo, up to ~44 s, with four read heads for stereo, ping-pong or multi-tap. time by hand, tap or clock; tape speed by cv at 1 v/oct, stop and reverse; an effects loop sits inside the delay.
jacksin 1 l · 2 r · 3 clock · 4 level · 5 speed cv · out 1/2 dry + delay · 3/4 delay only
default[ l ] feedback · [ l ] push = tap tempo
03sd multisamplesampler · poly
eight-voice, three-part multitimbral wav instrument from the card. up to three cv/gate pairs (gate voltage = velocity, 5 v max) or midi / i2c. velocity layers, round robins, chords + arps, eq, delay, scala / mts tuning.
jacksin pitch / gate pairs 1–3 · out stereo · mono mix
defaultin 3 → sustain
04sd 6 triggerssampler · drums
six drum voices from the card, each with its own trigger and sample folder: a far more capable i-8. velocity-sensitive triggers, velocity layers and round robins per sample. also played by midi / i2c; an es-5 adds more outputs.
jacksin 1–6 trigger voices 1–6 · out assignable
defaultno default mappings
05wav recorderutility · sd
records up to six channels of audio or cv to !RECORD/REC00001.WAV… at 48 / 96 khz, 16 / 24-bit, and plays back the last take. an auto-sampler captures multisampled instruments by itself.
jacksin 1 l · 2 r · 3–6 extra channels · 5 rec cv · 6 play cv · out 1/2 · 3/4 pairs
default[ l ] push = record · [ r ] push = play · turn ccw = locked
06multi-switchutility · routing
six dc-coupled switches, sequential or voltage-controlled. each picks one of six inputs and sends it to one of four outputs; shared outputs sum. crossfades run from click-free to long blends.
jacksin switch sources or control cvs · out switch targets
default[ l ] macro 1 · [ l ] push = macro 2
07looperaudio · looper
four loopers run like a two-button stomp box: record / overdub / clear and play / pause / mute act on the target loop. 8 / 16 / 32-bit, mono or stereo (~21 s each at 16-bit mono). fades, clock sync, reverse, half speed.
jacksin + out assignable per loop · monitor mix
default[ l ] push = record · [ r ] push = play
08dream machinevoice · drone
la monte young drones: a fundamental plus four tones tuned by prime ratios, wavetable voices (or a pure sine / triangle / square root), a gate per tone and a reverb. made to be played by hand.
jacksin 5 wave cv · out stereo · fundamental · its envelope
default[ l ] wave offset
09filter bankaudio · filter
eight parallel stereo filters in resonator, bandpass or multiband mode. levels by hand, cv, gate envelopes or an octature lfo; pitch by hand, cv or midi, so the resonators play chords.
jacksin stereo · optional cv + gate · out stereo · odd sum · even sum
defaultno default mappings
10poly wavetablevoice · poly synth
a full eight-voice wavetable synth: two envelopes, a filter and an lfo per voice, delay and chorus. cv/gate (velocity gates) or midi, chords + arps, stereo, mono or one output per voice, scala / mts.
jacksin 2 pitch · 4 gate · 3 sustain · 5 wave · 6 filter · out stereo · mono mix
default[ l ] wave offset
11granulatoraudio · granular
grains from live input or the card: record a ~31 s buffer or stream it. notes (cv/gate, midi, i2c) spawn grain clouds; three drone voices turn it into an effect. size, pitch, pan and spawn can all be randomised.
jacksin 5 l · 6 r · 1 delay mean · 3 record · 2 pitch · 4 gate · out 1/2 mix · 3/4 grains
default[ l ] delay mean · [ l ] push = record
12multi fxaudio · fx
stereo eq, pitch, delay and reverb at once, in series or parallel (feedback is possible), plus a variable sample rate under cv for lo-fi and pitch tricks.
jacksin 1 l · 2 r · 3 vsr · 4 pitch shift · 5 reverb time · out mix · aux
defaultin 5 → reverb time
13poly excitervoice · plucked
an eight-voice plucked / struck synth: a short burst (built-in, wav from the card or live input) rings a resonator. strum mode with aftertouch, delay + chorus, chords and arps, scala / mts.
jacksin 5 tone · 6 damp · 1 pressure · 3 sustain · 2 pitch · 4 gate · out stereo · mono
defaultinputs mapped as listed
14quad envelopecv · envelopes
four dahdsr envelopes with optional dc-coupled vcas, gated, one-shot or looping, synced to clock or midi. shapes are separate from generators, so four envelopes can share one shape.
jacksin gates / triggers + clock · out 4 envelopes (or vca’d signals)
default[ l ] push = trigger the current envelope
15convolveraudio · reverb
real-time convolution with an impulse from the card or recorded live: realistic rooms, or any two sounds smeared together. mono, mono-to-stereo or stereo trade against impulse length. record your own impulses ([ l ] push or the trigger), save them to impulses/, and live update re-convolves as you edit.
jacksin 1 l · 2 r · 3 / 4 rec l r · rec trigger · out 1 / 2 · 3 / 4 impulse
default[ l ] push = generate impulse
16chord enginecv · harmony
one pitch cv in, four harmonised pitch cvs out, in shape or satb voice-leading mode with root by cv or midi. also a poly voice distributor and arpeggiator; scala / mts and built-in vco calibration. satb = soprano / alto / tenor / bass, with the cantus firmus choosing which voice follows the input; adds a melodic minor scale and can send the chord out as midi.
jacksin 2 pitch · 4 gate · 1 root cv · 3 root gate · 6 chord gate · 5 cal · out 1–4 pitch
defaultno default mappings
17microtunerutility · tuner
a tuner for up to four signals at once, in 12-tet or any scala / mts scale. double-click [ v ] for the tuning display, which adapts to one, two or four inputs; auto visuals (algorithm menu) brings it up when idle.
jacksin 4 audio · out thru or fixed volts
defaultno default mappings
18trackercv · pitch follow
follows the pitch and envelope of incoming audio and turns them into cvs, a harmonised chord of cvs, or pitch-shifted copies of the input. shape, satb or midi harmony; scala / mts. calibration: patch each vco into input 5 and run it from the algorithm menu.
jacksin audio · root cv · 5 cal · out envelope · pitch cv · mix l / r
defaultno default mappings
19macro oscillatorvoice · braids
four independent braids (mutable instruments) with their own outputs, identical at 96 khz. any input can drive any instance; over midi / i2c they play as a four-voice poly synth. the algorithm menu holds the per-instance settings (inputs, flatten, drift, signature, midi option, shared settings); no meta mode needed, just map a cv to the model.
jacksin 2 pitch · 3 fm · 4 trigger · 5 timbre · 6 colour · out 1–4 one each
defaultset in the algorithm menu
20resonatorvoice · rings
mutable instruments rings with a stereo mix of the odd, even and dry signals. played by cv/gate, midi or i2c; set an input parameter to 0 and it strums or excites itself, like an unpatched jack on the original. midi / i2c modes: pitch, strum, or both. out 1/2 stereo mix · 3 resonator mix · 4 mono.
jacksin audio · brightness · damping · structure · position · out l / r · res · mono
defaultno default mappings
21macro oscillator 2voice · plaits
four independent plaits (v1.2 code) with free input assignment. midi / i2c gives round-robin polyphony; with no trigger input set, the envelope is skipped and it drones, as on the original. fm banks: dx7 sysex in FMSYX/ loads into the six-operator model.
jacksin pitch cvs 1–4 + assignable · out 1–4 one per instance
defaultset in the algorithm menu
22midi playercv · sequencer
plays .mid files from the card to the midi breakout and to cv / gates, on its own clock, analogue clock or midi clock. four pitched converters + a drum converter, more gates with an es-5; _ONESHOT files don’t loop. per-track mute, file restart modes, reset input as reset or run / stop, clock read as 24 ppqn or note values.
jacksin 1 clock · 2 reset · 6 play · 5 cal · out 1 / 3 pitch · 2 / 4 gate
defaultin 6 → play
23poly fmvoice · fm
eight-voice, four-part multitimbral six-operator fm (the plaits engine). loads dx7 voice banks from the card and works with dx7 editor / librarian apps. each timbre has its own bank, voice, output, gain / pan, brightness and envelope scale; timbres follow the gate inputs or adjacent midi channels. voice banks live in FMSYX/ on the card.
jacksin pitch / gate pairs · 3 sustain · out 1–4
defaultin 3 → sustain
24spectral freezeaudio · freeze
captures the spectrum of a moment (fft) and holds it forever, then replays it at any speed or pitch. four freeze voices mix to stereo with delay and reverb; etherization strips it to pure tones. double-click [ l ] to grab a new moment and crossfade to it.
jacksin 1 audio · 2 freeze gate · 5 6 3 4 voice 1–4 · out 1/2 mix · 3/4 dry
default[ l ] freeze target · [ l ] push = freeze toggle
25quad quantizercv · quantizer
four quantizers, independent or sharing a scale: nearest, warped or mapped modes, transpose and shift, key + scale masks, scala / mts, gate-sampled or free, and vco calibration. each cv goes: sample › transpose in › lookup scale › shift › output scale › transpose out › calibration. quantized notes can go out as midi; key + scale masks drop notes from the keyboard map.
jacksin 1–4 pitch cvs · 5 cal · out 1–4 quantized
defaultno default mappings
26quadraphonic mixeraudio · quad
six inputs placed around a four-speaker field by x / y or angle / radius, with spinners that circle sources round the listener. cv, midi or i2c positioning; with a cvm-8 all six sources can move at once.
jacksin 1–4 audio · 5 x / angle 1 · 6 y / radius 1 · out 1 fl · 2 fr · 3 rl · 4 rr
defaultin 5 / 6 → input 1 position
